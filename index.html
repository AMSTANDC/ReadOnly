How it works — Google Drive

Install Google Drive for Desktop (free) and sign in with the same Google account on every computer. The JSON file syncs automatically — no sharing, no links, no permissions needed.

Step 1 — Install Google Drive
Download Google Drive for Desktop from
drive.google.com/drive/download
Install and sign in with your Google account on every PC.
Step 2 — Link the site file
On the Arndale PC — open the Entry app, click 📁 Link Google Drive File, navigate to your local Google Drive folder and save the JSON there.
Google Drive syncs it to the cloud instantly.
Step 3 — Link here
On your Manager PC — click 📁 Link Local Google Drive File below and navigate to the same JSON file in your local Google Drive folder.
Click ↺ Reload any time for live data.
💡 Tip: The Google Drive folder on your PC is usually at C:\Users\[You]\Google Drive\My Drive\ on Windows or ~/Google Drive/My Drive/ on Mac.
